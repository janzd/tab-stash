EXPERIMENTAL

Previews while you browse

Help try a quieter way to collect screenshots. After you stay on a page for a moment, TabStash can save a small preview without switching tabs or moving focus.

Only ordinary web pages in the focused window are eligible. Sleeping, loading, audible, and split-view tabs are skipped. Previews may contain private page content; they stay on this device.

The temporary cache holds up to 40 previews, capped at 4 MiB. Previews older than one hour are ignored. The cache clears when Chrome restarts or the extension reloads. This experiment does not create automatic decks or change your backups.
